# Hydraulic lockout and error E001

> Demo corpus written for this project, not CAT documentation. E001 is a demo code, not a real CAT code.

## Hydraulic lockout lever
The hydraulic lockout lever sits beside the seat. Pulled up or locked, it cuts pilot pressure, so the joysticks cannot move the boom, stick, bucket or swing. Lock it every time before you release the seat belt or leave the seat. Unlock it only when you are seated, belted and ready to work.

## Error E001: lockout switch signal lost
Error E001 (DEMO-E001) means the machine has lost the signal from the hydraulic lockout lever switch. The machine can no longer tell whether the lever is locked or unlocked. It is shown for the CAT 320 excavator in this demo. The lever itself may still work; the problem is that the system cannot confirm its position.

## What to do when E001 appears
When E001 appears and the lockout switch signal is lost, treat the hydraulics as unlocked. Stop working, lower the bucket fully to the ground, move the controls to neutral and lock the lever anyway. Shut the engine off before you leave the cab. Report the switch fault to maintenance and do not return to work until it has been checked.

## Why E001 matters for Exit Guard
Exit Guard checks that the hydraulics are locked before you leave the seat. With E001 active, that check reads Unknown, and Unknown always counts as not safe. Exit Guard will warn on every exit until the switch is repaired, so the only safe exit is bucket on the ground and engine off.

## E001 compared with other lockout problems
If the lever feels loose, will not latch, or the joysticks still move the implement with the lever locked, that is a mechanical fault, not E001. Stop, lower the bucket, shut down and report it: never work with a lockout that does not hold.
