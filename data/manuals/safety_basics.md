# Operator safety basics

> Demo corpus from generic public safety practice — not CAT documentation.

## Daily walkaround
Park on level ground, lower the bucket, lock the hydraulics and shut the engine off first. Look for leaks under the machine, check fluid levels, tracks or tyres, pins, hoses and the bucket. Clean steps and windows, and check the seat belt, lights, horn and backup alarm. Report any safety defect and do not operate until it is fixed.

## Safe shutdown and parking
Park on firm, level ground away from edges, overhead lines and traffic. Lower the bucket fully to the ground, move controls to neutral, engage the hydraulic lockout and apply the parking brake. Let the engine idle briefly after heavy work, then shut it off. Remove the key if you leave the machine.

## Mounting and dismounting
Always face the machine and keep three points of contact: two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand. Use the steps and handholds, never the controls or door. Do not jump down or carry things while climbing. Wet or muddy steps are slippery; take extra care.

## Leaving the cab
Before you release the seat belt, check the bucket is on the ground, the hydraulics are locked out and the engine is off. Leaving with the bucket raised or hydraulics unlocked means the implement can move or drop.

## Coupler checks
After changing an attachment, confirm the coupler is fully locked. Do a test: curl the bucket and press it lightly against the ground to check it cannot come loose. Never swing a load over people.

## Overheating response
If the engine, hydraulic or transmission temperature rises, reduce load and let the machine idle so it can cool. Check coolers and radiators for debris when the engine is off. Never open a hot radiator cap. If a stop-level warning appears, stop safely, lower the implement and shut down.

## Stop-level warnings
A stop-level warning such as very low oil pressure or engine overheating means stop in a safe place, lower the implement, lock the hydraulics and shut down. Report it and do not restart until maintenance has checked it.

## Seat belt
Wear the seat belt whenever the engine is running and you are in the seat. In a rollover, the belt keeps you inside the protective cab.

## People near the machine
Before moving or swinging, check mirrors and cameras and sound the horn. Stop if anyone enters the swing area or walks behind the machine. Only continue when they are clear and you have eye contact.
